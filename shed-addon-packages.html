Shed Add-On Packages

Adding a package to your Lofted Barn or Utility Shed is an ideal way to give your shed a significant upgrade at a reasonable price.

Below are all eight packages we offer, each fully customizable in size, siding, and color to match the building you're building it onto.

Package 01

Deluxe Playhouse Package

  • 9-lite walk-in door
  • Five single-pane 2′x3′ windows, with three in the bay front
  • Wrap-around porch with three railing sections and four posts
  • 8ft walls (Utility Shed version)

Available widths

12′ wide 14′ wide 16′ wide

Package 02

Playhouse Package

  • 9-lite walk-in door
  • 4ft porch with two posts
  • Three single-pane 2′x3′ windows
  • Vaulted Gable upgrade available — adds a crow's-foot architectural detail with a 5/12 or 7/12 pitch (8′–12′ wide only, 4′ max porch)

Available widths

8′ wide 10′ wide 12′ wide 14′ wide 16′ wide

Package 03

Side Porch Package

  • 9-lite walk-in door
  • 4ft side porch with railing
  • One post
  • Two windows
  • Double barn doors

Available widths

8′–16′ wide

Package 04

Center Porch Package

  • 9-lite walk-in door
  • Two windows
  • 12′x4′ standard porch
  • Two posts and two railing sections
  • Minimum size 12′x24′

Available widths

12′ wide 14′ wide 16′ wide

Package 05

Garage Door Package

  • Roll-up garage door (8ft or 9ft)
  • Solid walk-in door
  • One window
  • 12in on-center floor joists
  • Diamond-plate threshold

Available widths

10′–16′ wide

Package 06

Utility Shed Dormer Package

  • 7/12 pitch roof
  • Three 10in x 29in horizontal windows
  • 7ft walls
  • Only available in 8ft, 10ft, or 12ft widths

Available widths

8′ wide 10′ wide 12′ wide

Package 07

Lofted Dormer Package

  • Three 10in x 29in horizontal windows
  • Available on any Lofted Barn size

Package 08

Gable Dormer Package

  • One 18in octagonal window

Frequently asked questions

Shed Add-On Package FAQs

Where can I find shed color codes and custom colors?

Our standard colors are pulled from the Sherwin-Williams A100 base library. You can also browse and search custom colors using our online 3D Builder.

How long are delivery lead times?

In-stock buildings typically deliver in 1–2 weeks. Custom orders typically take 4–6 weeks, weather permitting.

Does Lincoln County Buildings offer build-on-site?

Yes, build-on-site is available in many areas. The customer is responsible for paint, stain, or sealing after construction.

How do I cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible at (775) 962-1302 — cancellation fees may apply depending on order status.
