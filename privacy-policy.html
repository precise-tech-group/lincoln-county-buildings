Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, request a quote, or contact our team, we may collect information you provide directly — such as your name, mailing address, email address, phone number, and details about the building you're interested in. We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type and pages visited, to help us maintain and improve the site.

2. Text Messaging (SMS) Program

If you opt in to receive text messages from us — for example, by submitting your phone number on a quote or contact form — you may receive messages related to your inquiry, order status, or delivery updates. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or get help by replying HELP. Opt-out requests are processed within 10 business days. We do not sell or share your mobile information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, process orders, coordinate delivery, and communicate important updates about your purchase. We may also use it to improve our website and customer service.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary details with trusted partners who help us operate our business — such as the manufacturer (Old Hickory Buildings & Sheds) for order fulfillment, delivery drivers for scheduling, and rent-to-own financing partners when you choose that payment option. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the information below. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time.

6. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the personal information you share with us. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
