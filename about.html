About Lincoln County Buildings

Lincoln County Buildings sits on Buckhorn Ranch Rd in Alamo, Nevada — the kind of town where a handshake still means something, and a shed still needs to outlast the person who ordered it.

We're the authorized Old Hickory Buildings & Sheds dealer for Lincoln County and the surrounding area — the local face of a manufacturer that's spent decades building the old-fashioned way. Everything on our lot, and everything we deliver, carries that promise forward.

Two ways to shop

Choose your size, siding, roofing, color, and add-ons, and watch it come together in real time — then bring your design straight to our team for ordering.

The manufacturer behind every build

Old Hickory Buildings & Sheds.

For over 25 years, Old Hickory Buildings & Sheds has stood for quality craftsmanship, dependability, and customer satisfaction — a trusted leader in the portable storage industry since 1997, building using traditional, old-fashioned country craftsmanship handed down through generations in many Amish and Mennonite communities. Lincoln County Buildings is proud to be their authorized dealer for Alamo, Nevada and the surrounding area, offering sheds, barns, garages, cabins, and utility buildings — all fully customizable to your needs. Every design also meets IBC standards for Minor Storage Facilities (Occupancy Category I), with a full-time engineer available for custom plans and higher wind ratings when you supply the required anchors.

Free delivery & set-up

What to expect on delivery day

  • No site prep needed if the site is accessible and within 3ft of level
  • Free setup with customer-provided blocks plus driver-supplied shims — the driver can also supply blocks for a fee
  • The first 30 miles of delivery are free
  • In-stock buildings deliver in about 5–7 days
  • Custom orders deliver in about 30 days, weather permitting

Does Lincoln County Buildings ever offer a reduction in prices?

Yes — we offer varying deals and discounts throughout the year. Call us at (775) 962-1302 to ask about current offers.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. An active military ID is required, and the discount applies to new buildings only.

Do you offer interior finishings like electrical, plumbing, insulation, or drywall?

No. We recommend an experienced local contractor for interior finish work — improper modifications can void your warranty.

Can you convert my shed into a tiny home?

No — our buildings are not intended as living space. We recommend working with a licensed contractor for residential conversions.
