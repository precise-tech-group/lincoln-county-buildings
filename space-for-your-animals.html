Space For Your Animals.

From a run-in stall for horses and livestock to a backyard coop for your flock, we build dedicated structures that keep your animals sheltered and your feed and tack organized.

Two building families, each sized to your herd or flock and fully customizable in siding, roofing, and add-ons.

Shelter & stall

Animal Shelters

An open-front run-in style shelter sized to your herd, with a durable pressure-treated base and kick boards that stand up to daily wear.

  • 4x6in pressure-treated perimeter base
  • 1-1/4in pressure-treated kick boards, roughly 4ft high
  • Standard 8ft stall opening
  • One stall per 12ft of length
  • Optional Tack Room add-on (requires 4ft+ extra length) with tongue-and-groove engineered panel flooring, an OSB divider wall, and a 3ft or 4ft wooden barn door

For the flock

Chicken Coops, built in detail

A gable-roofed coop with everything your flock needs built in. Tap or hover the markers to see what's included.

01 — Egg Collection

4 Nesting Boxes

Four built-in nesting boxes with an exterior hatch for easy egg collection — upgradeable to an extended set of 6 nesting boxes on 12ft+ coops.

02 — Roosting

Roosting Ladder

A built-in roosting ladder gives your flock a place to perch overnight, right alongside the nesting boxes.

03 — Flock Access

Small Chicken Exit Door

A dedicated small exit door lets your flock come and go from an attached run without opening the main barn door.

04 — Entry

3ft Wooden Barn Door

A 3ft wooden barn door for full access, plus two 24″x27″ windows for light and ventilation. 5/12 gable roof with a 6ft3in wall.

How long are delivery lead times?

In-stock buildings typically deliver in 1–2 weeks. Custom orders typically take 4–6 weeks, weather permitting.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. An active military ID is required, and the discount applies to new buildings only.

Where can I find shed color codes and custom colors?

Our standard colors are pulled from the Sherwin-Williams A100 base library. You can also browse and search custom colors using our online 3D Builder.

Does Lincoln County Buildings offer build-on-site?

Yes, build-on-site is available in many areas. The customer is responsible for paint, stain, or sealing after construction.
