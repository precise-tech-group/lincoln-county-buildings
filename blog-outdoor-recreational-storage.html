Storage Shed Ideas for Bikes, ATVs & Outdoor Equipment

If your garage is overflowing with bikes, ATVs, and the rest of your outdoor gear, a dedicated storage shed can free up space and keep everything organized, protected, and ready to go.

Why Proper Storage Matters

Leaving bikes, ATVs, and riding gear exposed to the weather — or crowded into a garage corner — shortens their lifespan and makes them an easy target for theft. A dedicated, lockable building keeps your equipment protected from sun, rain, and snow while keeping it out of sight from the street.

Outdoor Bike Storage: Choose the Right Shed

The best shed style for bike storage depends on how many bikes you're storing and what else you want to keep alongside them.

Utility Sheds

A Utility Shed is a great fit for a smaller bike collection or a single family's worth of gear. With double barn doors and optional shelving, it's easy to roll bikes in and out while keeping helmets and accessories organized on a shelf above.

Lofted Barns

If you need to store bikes alongside larger items — or you simply have more gear to fit — a Lofted Barn adds overhead storage space so bulky, infrequently used items can go up top while bikes and ATVs stay on the ground floor, ready to roll out the door.

Store More Than Just Bikes

A well-planned shed can hold far more than two wheels. Depending on your building's size and door package, you can comfortably store:

  • ATVs
  • Side-by-Sides / UTVs
  • Golf Carts
  • Dirt Bikes
  • Small Motorcycles
  • Helmets and Riding Gear

For larger vehicles like UTVs and side-by-sides, a wider door opening makes all the difference. Visit our Shed Add-On Packages page to see our garage door package options, or request a free quote and tell us what you're storing — we'll help you size the right building.
