Purchasing a Shed Over Time

Not everyone wants to pay for a new building in full up front — and you don't have to. Our Rent-to-Own program lets you spread the cost of a shed, barn, or garage over simple monthly payments.

What Are Rent-to-Own Sheds?

Rent-to-Own lets you make simple monthly payments on your building with no credit check and no long-term contract. Once your payments are complete, the building is yours to keep — no further payments, no balloon fee, no catch.

This works differently than a traditional self-storage unit rental. With self-storage, you pay every month indefinitely and never own anything — the unit belongs to the storage facility the entire time. With Rent-to-Own, every payment moves you closer to owning the physical building outright, so once it's paid off, it's permanently yours.

Rent-to-Own agreements through Lincoln County Buildings come with several advantages:

  • No credit check required
  • Instant approval
  • 90 days same as cash
  • No early payoff penalties
  • Month-to-month agreement

Whether you're not ready to pay in full or simply prefer to budget your purchase over time, Rent-to-Own is worth considering.
